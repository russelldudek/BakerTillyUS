Value Conversion Review

A practical decision surface for moving a Consulting AI initiative from enthusiasm to an evidence-backed portfolio decision.

ConditionQuestion leadership must answerEvidence
Work redesigned

What changes in the actual consulting workflow, handoffs, knowledge access, exceptions, and review?

Current/future workflow; user observation; exception map; quality criteria

Economics improved

Which economic mechanism changes - quality, capacity, speed, pricing, leverage, utilization, risk, or growth?

Baseline; unit of work; cost-to-serve; capacity; pricing hypothesis; margin implications

Judgment protected

Where must professional accountability, confidentiality, source trace, and human review remain explicit?

Authority map; control points; data boundaries; evaluation; escalation; audit trail

Adoption sustained

What makes the new behavior easier, relevant, reinforced, and locally owned?

Role expectations; workflow placement; manager coaching; repeat use; confidence; support burden

Decision rule

A technically feasible use case becomes a consulting priority when all four conditions support a credible value case.
