# Baker Tilly Brand Intelligence

## Identity and provenance
- Employer identity: Baker Tilly wordmark, locally committed at `assets/brand/baker-tilly-logo.svg`.
- Source: Wikimedia Commons file page for BakerTilly-Logo.svg, which identifies Baker Tilly as the author/source and classifies the simple text/geometric logo as public-domain textlogo while noting trademark protection. The current Baker Tilly US site confirms that the name and associated logo are used under license from Baker Tilly International Limited.
- Use: nominative identification only. The mark is not redrawn, merged, animated, or presented as candidate-owned work.

## Color system
- Official/source asset charcoal: `#2B2A29`, extracted from the committed SVG.
- Source-sampled current web accent: lime/chartreuse family represented by `#D4EF39`, sampled from current Baker Tilly digital surfaces and used as a supporting recognition cue.
- Candidate-original accent: `#60D8D1`, restricted to the Value Conversion Equation thread and decision-state emphasis.
- Neutral system: white, near-black, cloud gray, and line gray.

## Typography
The current Baker Tilly web experience uses a direct, contemporary neo-grotesk sans-serif character. No proprietary font binaries are copied. The campaign uses a licensed/system stack led by Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, and Arial, with tight display tracking and restrained document typography.

## Visual grammar
The employer layer uses high contrast, large editorial sans-serif type, white/charcoal fields, sharp edges, chartreuse emphasis, and generous space. Candidate originality comes from the role-derived Value Conversion Equation—not from modifying the employer mark.

## Public research signals applied
- Baker Tilly publicly describes consulting value as shifting from effort to outcomes and the consultant as an architect of better decisions.
- The July 20, 2026 Baker Tilly/Ode/Anthropic initiative emphasizes AI systems built around professional expertise, methodologies, standards, and governed delivery.
- Current Baker Tilly AI guidance emphasizes quality data, human review, governance, and operational readiness.

## Independence
Every recruiter-facing surface identifies the work as an independent candidate vision and avoids implying Baker Tilly sponsorship, approval, or authorship.
